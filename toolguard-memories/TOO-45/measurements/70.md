# Ticket 70 remaining scope, measured 2026-08-21

Main defect (`parse_failures` dropped by `with_layer_rules_replaced`) is **fixed** in `05f786d`. Two items remain, **both reproduce**.

## 1. Unconditional double-wrapping — confirmed

```
wrap_tool_pattern('Bash', 'git:*')       -> 'Bash(git:*)'
wrap_tool_pattern('Bash', 'Bash(git:*)') -> 'Bash(Bash(git:*))'
wrap_tool_pattern('Read', 'Read(/**)')   -> 'Read(Read(/**))'
```

`wrap_tool_pattern` lives in **`toolguard/config.py`**, not in `edit_proposal.py` as the ticket's framing implies. A hand-authored `--edits` JSON carrying an already-wrapped pattern yields an inert rule with **no error**.

## 2. Caption versus enacted — confirmed, and structurally exact

| dataclass | fields | role |
|---|---|---|
| `EditProposal` | `action`, `tool`, `rationale`, `edits`, `origin` | what a consumer **renders** |
| `RuleEdit` | `tool`, `list_type`, `provenance`, `removed_patterns`, `added_patterns` | what `apply_edits` **enacts** |

**`apply_edits` never references `proposal.tool`** — verified by reading its source. So `EditProposal.tool`/`action`/`rationale` are pure caption: nothing reads them when applying, and nothing checks them against `edits`. Two proposals captioned differently over one identical edit tuple are byte-identical in effect, which is how a caption reading *"tighten Bash"* enacted a `Read` broadening to `/**`.

## THE INTERACTION WITH 20a — noticed 2026-08-21, must not be missed

**20a's repair round adds a `verification: SafetyResult` field to `ConsolidationProposal` and carries it through `consolidation_to_edit_proposal` into `EditProposal`.** That is **one more caption field on the exact dataclass this ticket is about.**

It is defensible — `verification` is derived from the gate that produced those edits, so it is coherent *by construction*, unlike `tool`/`action`/`rationale`, which are separately authored. **But it enlarges the caption surface, and whatever cross-check ticket 70 introduces has to account for it.**

Two consequences:

- **Sequence 70 after 20a**, so the cross-check is designed against the final field set rather than being retrofitted.
- **Whoever implements 70 must be told `verification` exists and why it is not the same kind of field.** A cross-check that naively demands every `EditProposal` field correspond to something in `edits` would reject it wrongly.
